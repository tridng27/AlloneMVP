What to know before you roll out

Answers to common questions about bringing AllOne Omni into your customer care workflow.

What does Early Access mean?

It means the channels and features in the table above are already running with real users, while the in-progress items are not yet. You can use what's live right away.

Does the AI make up answers?

No. The AI only picks from scripts your team has written. When nothing matches, the conversation is handed to a staff member.

Do I need the CRM to use Omni?

It's not required. But alongside the CRM, conversations attach to the right customer profile instead of sitting apart.

Will staff have to learn a new tool?

The main screen is an inbox with a list of conversations. Training is part of the rollout, along with writing your first set of scripts.

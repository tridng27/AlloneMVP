Why it isn't instant self-service sign-up

An empty account with sample data tells you nothing about your own business. We trade a few days of preparation so the trial reflects your team's real work.

Before you sign up

Do I need to give payment details?

No. Sign-up only needs your contact details and team size so we can prepare the right configuration.

How should I prepare my data?

The Excel or Google Sheets files you already use are enough. No need to clean them up first; removing duplicates is part of our job.

Which products can I try?

The CRM is the most common starting point. If you're interested in Omni or LMS, mention it when you sign up so we can configure them too.

What happens after the trial?

You get a quote based on the scope you actually ran, not a hypothetical plan. See how pricing works first.

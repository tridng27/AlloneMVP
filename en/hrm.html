Six modules, one management system

Each module handles one area, and all share the employee directory, permissions and action history.

Employee profiles and permissions

Everyone gets the right account and the right access.

  • Centralized employee profiles
  • Create accounts and email login details automatically
  • Action-level permissions, grouped by department and job title

Requests and approvals

Ten request types through one approval flow.

  • Leave, late arrival, early departure, work from home, overtime, teaching schedule swaps
  • Reimbursements and payments with invoice photos
  • Approve or reject with notes; tickets older than 7 days close automatically

Monthly payroll

Payslips calculated from operational data instead of re-entered.

  • Import timesheet files to calculate pay
  • Teacher and teaching assistant pay based on rate tables and actual teaching hours
  • Office staff pay by branch

Goals and work

Each department's goals tied directly to daily work.

  • Objectives, Key Results and action plans following OKR
  • Kanban board and daily progress check-ins
  • Rolled up by department and company-wide

Internal announcements

Important news reaches the right people instead of getting lost in group chats.

  • Send announcements company-wide or by group
  • Announce system maintenance in a single step
  • Personal inbox separate from general announcements

Sign-in and security

Fast access without losing control.

  • Sign in with Google
  • Self-service password reset via email
  • Store and assign teaching platform accounts (Zoom…) to each teacher

Goals and work on the same board

The goals module runs on OKR: objectives break into Key Results, and Key Results break into action plans with an owner, deadline and progress.

  • Monthly OKR cycles

    One tab per month, filtered by department, owner and time range.

  • A Kanban board for each Key Result

    Action plans move through Backlog, In Progress, In Review and Done, with priority, start date, deadline and percent complete.

  • Cross-department tasks

    Assign work to someone in another department while keeping it tied to your own Key Result.

  • Daily check-ins and weekly plans

    Everyone updates their progress each day, and managers instantly see who has and hasn't checked in.

See the whole organization, and AI points out what to fix

Managers see progress by department and company-wide on one screen, while the AI layer reads that same data to score and suggest.

Manager View

The full picture of each department and the whole company for the month.

  • Overall progress, check-in rate, Key Result status
  • Action plans completed out of the total
  • A Key Result table: progress, owner, and task count in each column
  • Which departments are behind, so you can step in in time

AI effectiveness scoring

An Effectiveness (AI) score for each department, next to its Efficiency metric.

  • Explains why a score is high or low, based on real check-ins and progress
  • Flags slow Key Results and tasks not clearly tied to a goal
  • Click “Rescore (AI)” after updating

Suggested improvements

AI suggests how to reorganize work; managers decide whether to apply it.

  • Suggests splitting overdue Key Results into smaller pieces
  • Suggests reassigning owners
  • Every suggestion is only a suggestion; nothing changes your data automatically
OKR Assistant

Ask about OKR progress in plain language and let the AI draft an action plan for a Key Result. The owner approves it before it's applied.

One management cycle in 4 steps

The default flow. Departments, permissions, request types and pay formulas are configured for each business.

  1. 1
    Step 01 Administrator

    Set up the organization

    Create employee profiles and accounts, assign permissions by department, and set up pay rate tables.

  2. 2
    Step 02 Manager

    Set goals and assign work

    Set OKRs for each department, break them into action plans and assign owners.

  3. 3
    Step 03 System

    Daily operations

    Employees check in and submit requests; the system routes approvals and compiles progress and payroll data.

  4. 4
    Step 04 AI

    AI reviews and suggests

    AI scores each department's effectiveness; managers review the suggestions and decide on changes.

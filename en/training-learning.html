Three things this platform does for your team

Not one more piece of software, but exactly the data and workflows this problem needs, brought together.

Timetables and conflict prevention

Schedule recurring classes by shift, and the system checks instructor and room conflicts before saving.

  • Recurring classes by shift and by week
  • Conflict checks for instructors, assistants and rooms
  • Create online classrooms and save session recordings

Attendance and teaching hours

Take attendance per session, and teaching hours add up automatically in the payroll sheet instead of being retyped at month end.

  • Attendance sheet for every session
  • Teaching hours compiled automatically per instructor
  • Learner attendance compiled per course

Assignments, tests and materials

Assign, grade and store shared materials in the same system as your classes.

  • Multiple question types with suitable grading
  • Track completion along the course path
  • A shared materials library for multiple classes

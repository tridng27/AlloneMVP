What to know before rolling out AllOne LMS

Key information to help training centers and departments prepare before rollout.

Is it for training centers or internal training?

Both. Centers run by class and course; internal training departments run programs for employees. Same system, different configuration.

Do we have to use the CRM as well?

No. The LMS runs on its own. With the CRM, enrollment connects directly to classes, so learner details never need to be entered twice.

Which platform do online classes run on?

Through the Zoom integration, attached directly to sessions in the timetable. Learners join from their own schedule.

Can we import existing learner data?

Yes. Learner lists, classes and course history are imported from your existing files during configuration, before handover.

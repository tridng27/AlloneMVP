Four factors that decide the final figure

Subscription pricing depends on plan and scale of use. After the assessment, your business receives a specific quote for implementation, integration and customization.

01

Number of modules

Using the CRM alone differs from rolling out CRM, HRM, LMS or ERP together. You can start with the module you need and expand in phases.

02

Number of users

The size of the team accessing the system drives most of the cost. It can change as your business grows or scales back.

03

Level of customization

Configuring around existing workflows costs less than building custom workflows, data fields and permission models for each department.

04

System integrations

Connections to your call center, online meetings, email and existing systems are priced by the number and complexity of each connection.

Phased rollout

You don't need to switch everything on in the first month. Most businesses start with one product and one department, then expand once the team is comfortable, so costs track real usage.

Benefits included in every plan

Transparent, unified data with full ownership in your hands. AllOne assesses on site, trains your team and expands in phases without upfront cost commitments.

Before you request a quote

What's included in the listed price?

The subscription is priced per user or per learner. Implementation, training, digital transformation consulting and call charges are billed separately, as separate line items on the quote.

Which plan is right for me?

If your goal is to centralize data and standardize operations, start with the Standard plan. When you need more features or scale, AllOne will recommend the right plan. Custom workflows, permissions and integrations are quoted based on rollout scope.

Do I have to use every module?

No. Modules are switched on and billed separately. AZTravel currently runs only the CRM, while Liam Education uses the CRM together with the LMS.

How does monthly billing differ from yearly?

Listed prices apply to 12-month prepaid contracts. With monthly billing, the price per user or learner is 20% higher.

How soon will the system be running?

The software is handed over within 1 month of signing, followed by 3 months of training and technical support.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The Free plan lets you see how the system organizes lead and contact data before you decide on rollout scope.

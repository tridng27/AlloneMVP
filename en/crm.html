Is your business getting full value from its payroll?

Unresolved problems drive up staffing costs and drag down operational performance.

01

Broken operations

Sales keeps the customer file, accounting keeps the receivables file, customer care keeps the appointments file. None of them is wrong, but none of them match.

02

Time wasted reconciling data

Every week, staff have to cross-check multiple spreadsheets. Some businesses even hire part-timers or outsource just to handle this repetitive work.

03

Declining staff productivity

Full-time staff do their actual jobs while also entering data and compiling reports. Repetitive work keeps growing and eats into time for core work.

04

Lost customers

Customer care history lives in personal files and in the memory of former employees. New hires struggle to pick up with the very customers you already have.

What to know before you roll out

Answers to common questions so your business can start with AllOne confidently.

Can we migrate our old Excel data?

Yes. During configuration, our implementation team imports your existing customer data from Excel or Google Sheets and removes duplicates before handover.

How long does rollout take?

It depends on the number of modules and the level of customization. After the assessment session, we give you a concrete timeline for each phase instead of a generic number.

What if our team isn't tech-savvy?

Training is part of the rollout. The goal is for your team to run the system on its own after handover, not to call support every time something needs changing.

Do we have to use all three products?

No. Many businesses start with the CRM alone and add Omni or LMS when they actually need them. AZTravel currently uses only the CRM.

Does the AI email customers on its own?

No. Automated workflows are configured within your business's scope and access rights. Important decisions are still reviewed and approved by people.

How is pricing calculated?

By number of modules, number of users and level of customization. The pricing page lists exactly what determines the final figure.

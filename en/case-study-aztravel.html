How AZTravel Helped Two Branches Work From One Booking Source

A travel company in Da Nang and Hanoi moved tour bookings, customer profiles and hotel partner payables from multiple Google Sheets into one CRM.

Deployment profile

Industry
Travel and tourism
Locations
Da Nang and Hanoi
Services
Budget tours and hotel bookings
Products deployed
CRM

AZTravel unified booking data across two branches

Customers, bookings and partners are updated in one CRM, so both branches work from the same data source.

01

Two locations, two seasonal rhythms, one brand

AZTravel sells budget tours and hotel services in the two largest tourist regions of central and northern Vietnam. Competition in this segment comes down to price and response speed, so the sales team has to close fast and keep operating costs low.

The two branches run in parallel with their own customer bases and departure schedules, but sell under one brand and work with many of the same hotel partners. That makes keeping both sides' numbers in sync a regular part of the job.

02

Peak season is when double bookings and payable errors happen most

Booking, customer, hotel partner and payables information was managed in many Google Spreadsheets split by tour group and location. When customer volume was low, this still worked.

At peak season, it didn't. Sales had to cross-check sheets themselves to avoid double-booking seats on the same departure or recording the wrong payables with hotel partners. The company had to hire part-timers and outsource just to do this reconciliation during busy periods.

The knock-on effect was pressure on full-time staff. Already fully booked with consultations and customer care, they took on data checking as well, and turnover rose with it.

03

One CRM for customers, bookings and partners

AZTravel rolled out the CRM alone, with no other products. The goal was deliberately narrow: bring everything related to a booking into one place so there's nothing left to reconcile.

CRM

Centralized data

Customers, tour bookings and hotel partners are updated in real time instead of typed by hand into many sheets.

CRM

Departures and seats

Managed per tour, reducing the risk of both branches selling the same open seat.

CRM

Partner payables

Tracked right on the booking record, instead of checked by hand at month end.

One shared set of bookings so Da Nang and Hanoi see the same seat status, instead of each branch keeping its own sheet.

A peak-season day, before and after

Peak season is still peak season. The difference is that sales no longer has to stop and check whether the other branch's sheet is up to date.

On many spreadsheets

Before
  • Each branch kept its own availability sheet and had to check with the other before confirming a customer.
  • Payables with each hotel were rebuilt by hand at month end.
  • Customer conversation history lived in the owner's files and messages.

On one system

After
  • Staff at both branches see the same seat status for every departure.
  • Payables with each hotel attach directly to the booking that created them, so there's nothing to rebuild at month end.
  • Customer conversation history lives on the profile, so whoever takes over can read all of it.

Three changes the company has seen

Two branches, one data source

Da Nang and Hanoi work from the same bookings and customers, with no more two versions of the truth.

No more seasonal reconciliation

With spreadsheet matching gone, so is the need to hire part-timers or outsource that work.

Sales gets back to selling

Time once spent checking figures now goes to consulting and customer care, easing the pressure behind turnover.

Why this chain of problems repeats across businesses

AZTravel sells tours and Liam Education teaches English, but both ran into the same chain of problems. The only difference is the file names in Google Drive.

Unified data

Customer information, transactions and documents live on one platform instead of many spreadsheets kept by each department.

Less reliance on temporary staff

No more hiring extra people just to cross-check figures between spreadsheets.

Less pressure driving turnover

Cutting repetitive manual work is the most direct way to keep the staff you have.

Easy handovers

Work history lives in the system, so new people get up to speed fast without former staff walking them through it.

Decisions on current numbers

Reports come from live data, not last week's manual summary.

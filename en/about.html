03

The AllOne story

In 2026, two people with two different strengths looked at the same problem: why do Vietnamese businesses still run on scattered spreadsheets?

  1. 14.05.2026

    A meeting

    The meeting between Trí, who builds AI platforms, and Nam, who runs a business day to day, opened up a different perspective: pairing strong technical capability with a deep understanding of the everyday operational snags Vietnamese businesses face.

  2. Back then

    A problem with no answer yet

    When data is fragmented across many spreadsheets, each file may be right yet the figures never match, wasting reconciliation time and money. The solutions on the market were either too expensive or too rigid.

    • Money spent on reconciliation. Businesses hire part-timers or outsource just to match spreadsheets, instead of putting resources into revenue-generating work.
    • Missed revenue in peak season. Two branches can sell the same open slot because data on one side hasn't reached the other yet.
    • Great people stuck in repetitive work. The same person consults, teaches, handles admin and enters the same information in many places.
    • Broken customer history. When information lives in personal files and in the owner's memory, newcomers struggle to understand the full context.
    • Decisions arrive after the numbers. Leadership may wait days for a report while the real situation has already changed.

  3. The decision

    Unable to find the right solution, the two decided to build AllOne themselves.

    AllOne didn't start from a wish to build yet another packaged software product. The platform exists to solve a core problem: unifying data, standardizing workflows and putting AI to real use at a reasonable cost.

  4. 03.07.2026

    AllOne Education and Technology Company Limited is founded

    First registered with the Business Registration and Enterprise Finance Office, Hanoi Department of Finance. The goal: give small and medium businesses a solid management platform, strong enough to break through in the digital era without disrupting how they run today.

  5. Today

    Real operations, at real businesses

    Liam Education, a business English training center, and AZTravel, a travel and tourism company in Da Nang and Hanoi, run on AllOne every day.

Our partnership process

Control investment risk and bring technology into stable operation, one step at a time. Each step builds on the results of the one before.

  1. 01Before the quote

    Assess first, quote after

    The first conversation identifies where your data lives, who owns it and where workflows get stuck. That way, you only invest in the CRM, HRM, LMS or ERP modules you really need right now.

  2. 02In phases

    Phased rollout

    You get a proposed scope and cost for each phase, and can start with one problem or one department, then expand once your team is using it confidently.

  3. 03Within 1 month

    Standardize data and configure workflows

    Spreadsheet data is moved into a shared structure and de-duplicated. Routing rules, nurturing playbooks, KPIs and approval flows are configured around real operations. The system is handed over within 1 month of signing the contract.

  4. 04First 3 months

    Training so the system actually gets used

    Your team gets training and technical support for the first 3 months, including up to 3 days on site. AllOne monitors usage and works with you to fix the steps that aren't running smoothly yet.

Timelines follow current implementation terms. Specific scope is agreed in each phase's proposal.

06

Why choose AllOne

You're not just choosing software. You're choosing a way of operating: clear on budget, secure with information, and ownership of the technology always stays with your business.

Your business data is an asset. And a responsibility. Our responsibility is to protect it, and to keep it in your business's hands.

Budget

What customers get

Scope, cost and timeline are made clear before rollout.

  • The solution scope is defined from your real workflows and data, so you avoid paying for features you don't need yet.
  • Costs for each phase are made clear before rollout. Custom development or customization only happens after scope and a project quote are agreed.
  • The current plan targets handover within 1 month of signing, plus 3 months of training and technical support so your team is up and running quickly.
  • Your business can expand in phases, without rolling out every module from day one.

Data

Your control over your data

Your business keeps ownership of its data for as long as it uses the system.

  • Role-based permissions give each person access only to the data their work needs, reducing the risk of out-of-scope access.
  • Action logs and change history support tracing whenever you need to audit or hand over work.
  • You can choose to deploy on your own domain, on a private server or in the cloud, depending on your infrastructure needs.

AI and automation

Your control over AI and automation

Automation only runs when your business turns it on, so you choose exactly which workflows to automate.

  • AI acts only within the permissions it's granted, so your business controls what data and functions the AI can use.
  • Automated replies and tasks are logged so owners can review them when needed.
  • Your business can switch off an AI task when something looks wrong, to stop a workflow from running in unwanted ways.
  • Steps like quote approvals, over-limit discounts or off-script situations are routed to the owner based on thresholds your business sets.

How AllOne began

From real operational headaches to a platform that helps businesses work better

14.05.2026

Trí and Nam met in a small office on a day of heavy rain.

The shared conviction of our two founders

On 14 May 2026, Trí and Nam met in a small office on a day of heavy rain. That meeting sparked many conversations about how technology could solve very specific difficulties in the daily operations of Vietnamese businesses.

One builds AI platforms, the other runs a business day to day. From two different perspectives, both reached the same conclusion: technology only creates value when it helps businesses save time, reduce errors and serve customers better.

AllOne didn't start from a wish to build yet another piece of software. It grew from the need to help small and medium businesses connect their data, standardize their workflows and access AI at a reasonable cost, so they can compete in the era of digital transformation.

The business problem at the time

At the end of every month, many staff still sat up late opening five or six Google Sheets to check tuition, bookings and payables. Each file might be right, but the figures across files didn't agree, costing the business extra time checking before it could act.

When data is scattered, businesses pay for it in time, money and customer experience.

Money spent on reconciliation

Businesses hire part-timers or outsource just to match spreadsheets, instead of putting resources into revenue-generating work.

Missed revenue in peak season

Two branches can sell the same open slot because data on one side hasn't reached the other yet.

Great people stuck in repetitive work

The same person consults, teaches, handles admin and enters the same information in many places, leaving less time for customers and their actual expertise.

Broken customer history

When information lives in personal files and in the owner's memory, newcomers struggle to understand the full context, and customers have to repeat themselves.

Decisions arrive after the numbers

Leadership may wait days for a report while the real situation has already changed.

What troubled Nam and Trí most was the image of staff staying late to process figures while their families waited at home. Both believe a good system should give people their time back, so they can focus on customers, their expertise and life outside work.

The two of them searched for and compared many solutions. International platforms were capable but expensive. Many local products didn't fully solve the scattered-data problem. That's when they concluded that Vietnamese businesses needed a modern management platform that could be rolled out in phases and follow real workflows closely.

Unable to find the right solution, the two decided to build AllOne themselves.

The value businesses get today

AllOne brings customer, sales, inventory, HR and training data onto one platform. Businesses cut repetitive data entry, reduce discrepancies between departments and track activity on live data. The AI layer supports analysis and automates suitable tasks, within the scope the business sets.

What AllOne aims for isn't the number of features, but the time your team gets back for work that matters. Liam Education can focus more on consulting and teaching. The AZTravel team has more time for customers instead of reconciling data by hand.

That's the yardstick AllOne uses for every rollout.

Mission and vision

Mission

To help Vietnamese businesses own their data and workflows, so they make decisions on live figures, cut repetitive work and raise productivity with the support of AI.

For our customers, digital transformation only matters when teams use the system every day, data flows between departments and leadership sees problems early enough to act.

Vision

To become the unified management platform trusted by small and medium businesses in Vietnam, where business modules and AI assistants work on one data source to cut reconciliation time and speed up decisions.

Core values

Substance over form

You get a system measured by the number of workflows that actually run, not by accounts issued or features delivered. AllOne only commits to what can be implemented and verified.

One data source, one shared understanding

Information is updated where it originates and reused across the relevant modules. Departments argue less about the numbers and spend their time deciding what to do next.

AI assists, people decide

AI can help classify leads, summarize calls, draft content and analyze data. Your business still controls access, the scope of automation and the steps that need an owner's approval.

Software that follows real workflows

Data objects, statuses and approval flows are configured around how your business actually runs, so teams get comfortable faster and rarely need to change processes just to fit the tool.

Empowered to operate, less dependence

Your team is trained to use and manage the handed-over configuration on its own, so everyday changes happen faster without waiting on AllOne for every small request.

How we work with you

Four steps from our first conversation to your team using the system with confidence.

  1. 1
    Step 01 Before the quote

    Assess first, quote after

    The first conversation identifies where your data lives, who owns it and where workflows get stuck. That way, you only invest in the CRM, HRM, LMS or ERP modules you really need right now.

  2. 2
    Step 02 In phases

    Phased rollout

    You get a proposed scope and cost for each phase, and can start with one problem or one department, then expand once your team is using it confidently.

  3. 3
    Step 03 Within 1 month

    Standardize data and configure workflows

Our commitments

What customers get

Scope, cost and timeline are made clear before rollout.

  • The solution scope is defined from your real workflows and data, so you avoid paying for features you don't need yet.
  • Costs for each phase are made clear before rollout. Custom development or customization only happens after scope and a project quote are agreed.
  • The current plan targets handover within 1 month of signing, plus 3 months of training and technical support so your team is up and running quickly.
  • Your business can expand in phases, without rolling out every module from day one.

Your control over your data

Your business keeps ownership of its data for as long as it uses the system.

  • Role-based permissions give each person access only to the data their work needs, reducing the risk of out-of-scope access.
  • Action logs and change history support tracing whenever you need to audit or hand over work.
  • You can choose to deploy on your own domain, on a private server or in the cloud, depending on your infrastructure needs.

Your control over AI and automation

Automation only runs when your business turns it on, so you choose exactly which workflows to automate.

  • AI acts only within the permissions it's granted, so your business controls what data and functions the AI can use.
  • Automated replies and tasks are logged so owners can review them when needed.
  • Your business can switch off an AI task when something looks wrong, to stop a workflow from running in unwanted ways.
  • Steps like quote approvals, over-limit discounts or off-script situations are routed to the owner based on thresholds your business sets.

Where AI lives in AllOne

One AI layer, working inside each module and within the limits your business sets.

Chat assistant for delegating work

Delegate work to the system in plain language.

  • Start a task with a single request
  • Track the status of every request
  • Schedule tasks to run later

AI in sales

Customer profiles and calls are processed as soon as they happen.

  • Automatically compile the customer profile on intake
  • Summarize the outcome after every logged call
  • Ask questions and analyze live ad data

AI in omnichannel care

Omni replies on Messenger and Zalo OA from the scripts you write.

  • Instant replies from approved scripts
  • Hands off to staff when a question is off-script
  • Staff see what the AI replied alongside the customer profile

Drafting and analysis

The first draft comes from existing data; a person approves the final version.

  • Write context-aware customer outreach emails
  • Create presentation slides from existing content
  • Compile market research and business analysis

Knowledge base with semantic search

Internal documents in one place, searchable by content instead of file name.

  • Store slides, internal processes and teaching materials centrally
  • Find the exact passage inside a PDF
  • Restrict viewing by role, department or individual

You stay in control

AI and automation run only within the scope your business sets.

  • Automation only runs when your business turns it on
  • AI acts only within the permissions it's granted
  • Every automated reply and task is logged
  • Switch off any AI task instantly if something looks wrong

An AI request goes through 4 steps

Your business sets the approval thresholds; this is the default flow.

  1. 1
    Step 01 Human

    You assign the task

    Enter a request in plain language, or schedule the task to run on its own.

  2. 2
    Step 02 AI

    AI works within its permissions

    The AI reads only the data it's granted and returns a result or a draft.

  3. 3
    Step 03 Human

    The owner approves

    Quotes, over-limit discounts and off-script situations are routed to a person based on the thresholds you set.

  4. 4
    Step 04 System

    Logged and updated

    Results are saved to the right profile, with a log for later review.
